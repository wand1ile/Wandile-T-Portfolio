About Me

Hi, I'm Wandile Tshabalala, a Final Year Computer Science student with a deep curiosity for how technology works and how ideas become real world digital experiences.

Academically, I've developed a strong foundation through coursework in algorithms, data structures, discrete computational structures, computer organization, databases, networks, and mobile computing. I enjoy understanding systems at both a theoretical and practical level, from how algorithms behave to how applications feel in the hands of users.

I value growth, clarity and mastery. I like breaking down complex ideas into simple, understandable components, a mindset that helps both in learning and in building. I'm constantly exploring ways to improve my technical skills, deepen my understanding, and refine how I think about design, systems, and user experience.

My long term goal is to become a highly skilled individual in tech, working on impactful, high quality products at great companies. I'm driven by a desire for both technical excellence and meaningful contribution — building solutions that are not only efficient and robust, but genuinely useful to people.

I'm especially motivated by the idea of creating technology that solves real problems, scales effectively, and reflects thoughtful engineering.

Municipal Service Delivery Reporting Portal

A full-stack web application designed to improve accountability and transparency in municipal service delivery. The platform enables residents to report and track community issues such as potholes, water outages, power failures, and illegal dumping, while allowing municipal workers and administrators to manage, prioritise, and resolve requests through a structured workflow. Key features include ward-level geolocation, real-time request tracking, notifications, public service delivery dashboards, analytics, and role-based access control.
